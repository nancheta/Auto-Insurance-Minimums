Resources Center

Definitions, downloads, and legislative-ready materials.

Bodily Injury Liability (BI)

Helps pay for injuries an insured driver causes to other people in an at-fault crash.

Property Damage Liability (PD)

Helps pay for damage an insured driver causes to another person's vehicle or property.

Personal Injury Protection (PIP)

Provides specified benefits, often including medical expenses and lost wages, without first determining fault.

Uninsured Motorist (UM)

Provides protection when an injured person is struck by a driver with no applicable liability insurance.

Underinsured Motorist (UIM)

Provides additional protection when an at-fault driver's limits are insufficient.

Medical Payments (MedPay)

Helps pay covered medical expenses, generally without regard to fault.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does Florida show $40,000?

The Explorer follows the agreed methodology and displays Florida's supplied 10/20/10 components as a combined $40,000 value for comparison.

What does “Dashboard total” mean?

It is the sum of the numeric coverage fields represented in the dataset. It is a visualization measure, not a premium or legal equivalency.

How should this information be used?

Use it as a policy research starting point and confirm current requirements through the linked official state source.

Methodology

Transparent assumptions for state-to-state comparison.

The prototype is generated from the supplied auto minimums state by state.ods workbook. Numeric values are displayed in thousands of dollars. Blank fields are charted as zero, which may mean not required, not represented, or needing further interpretation.

Florida normalization: Florida is displayed as $40,000 in the comparison field, combining the supplied 10/20/10 components.

Verification: Requirements can change. Check the official state source before publication or reliance.
